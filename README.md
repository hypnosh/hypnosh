# Amit Sharma
### From viral puzzles to product strategy, still creating, still learning.

**15+ years building products across fintech, insurtech, consumer technology and digital platforms.**

I've worked across product, UX, technology and strategy, from creating **Klueless**, one of India's early viral online puzzles, to leading digital products in financial services and advising founders on product strategy.
